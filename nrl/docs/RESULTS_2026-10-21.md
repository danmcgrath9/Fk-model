# NRL model: first results (21 Oct 2026)

Every number below is from `bash nrl/build.sh --final` on 21 Oct 2026; the full tables are in this folder
(team_seasons.md, TRIES_STEP1.md, FIRST_TRY_STEP1.md, COUNTS_STEP1.md, LATE_CHANGES.md). The final seasons
were scored ONCE, after every setting was frozen. **No real odds were used anywhere**: The Odds API probe has
not run (the ODDS_API_KEY secret is empty), so every ROI is against a stand-in line and is an upper bound.

## Data held (all on the `nrl-history` release)

| Source | Held |
|---|---|
| Rugby League Project (uselessnrlstats) | every game and player-game 1998 to 19 Jun 2026 (results stop part-way through round 16 2026; later rows are fixtures) |
| Champion Data match centre | 2,629 games 2014 to the 2026 grand final, none missing: ~50 stats per player, score flow with try times, named reserves |
| Joined | 2,530 games linked to RLP; 99 games of 2026 (20 Jun to 4 Oct) added from Champion Data; 1,321 of 1,382 players linked (median agreement 100%) |
| NRL.com team-list articles | 1,105 articles 2009-2026. Tuesday's named lists parsed for 1,385 team-games (2009, 2010, 2013, 2014 part, 2018 part); 99% of names matched. From 2020 the archived pages show the match-day 18 only, so no Tuesday lists survive for 2019-2026 |

## Team model (bits per game, Squiggle's score; higher is better)

| seasons | home team always | ratings only | at the open | teams named |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2019-2022 (tuning) | 0.008 | 0.145 | 0.150 | 0.159 |
| 2023-2024 (confirm) | 0.016 | 0.090 | 0.088 | 0.091 |
| 2025-2026 (final, untouched) | 0.007 | 0.056 | 0.051 | 0.056 |

- The ratings carry nearly everything. Line-up information helped in the tuning seasons and not after: by
  the house rule (works on one set only = dropped), **the "at the open" line-up features are dropped**; at the
  open, price on ratings only. The "teams named" version ties ratings only in the confirm and final sets.
- 2025-26 were harder to predict for every version (margin miss 14.7 points against 13.6 before).
- Calibration (2019-22): predicted and actual P(home margin > k) agree within about 2 points from -18.5 to
  +18.5, totals likewise; but near a line of 0 the smooth error curve is 4 points too high (margins of 1-2 are
  lumpy), and the home side beat our own margin 47% of the time. Lines near zero need an empirical margin
  distribution before real money.
- No public NRL model archive exists to compare with (Squiggle is AFL only); the market comparison waits for odds.

**Late changes** (666 games with both Tuesday lists, 2009-2018): one margin model scored 0.042 bits fed last
game's 17, 0.050 fed Tuesday's list, 0.051 fed who took the field. 6.1% of named 1-17 did not play. So the
backtest's "teams named" (who played) is barely optimistic: Tuesday's list carries almost all of it.

## Anytime try scorer

| seasons | model log loss | position-aware stand-in | naive stand-in (last 10) | ROI vs position-aware at its fair price, 10% edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2019-2022 | 0.4229 | 0.4264 | 0.4375 | +22.9% (+17.9% to +27.9%) |
| 2023-2024 | 0.4373 | 0.4382 | 0.4486 | +18.1% (+7.3% to +30.3%) |
| 2025-2026 final | 0.4393 | 0.4411 | 0.4501 | +26.7% (+17.0% to +36.7%) |

Calibrated to within a point in every tenth. The model's improvement over a competent stand-in is small in
log loss; the ROI is at the stand-in's FAIR price, so it is the margin a real book could charge before the
edge is gone, and real anytime markets carry a margin. Against the naive stand-in the ROIs (+54% to +84%) mean
only that last-10 strike rates are a poor price. First try scorer: the model beats a position-only share of the
first try in every set (3.163 against 3.225 in the final set, -log p of the actual scorer); real first-try
markets carry big margins, so no ROI is quoted as meaningful.

## Run metres and tackles (Champion Data, 2014 on)

| seasons | run metres, 56%+ at 1.87: hit / ROI | against the smarter stand-in | tackles, 56%+: hit / ROI | against the smarter stand-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020-2021 | 64.4% / +20.5% | 64.4% / +20.4% | 68.8% / +28.7% | 65.9% / +23.3% |
| 2022-2023 | 62.3% / +16.6% | 61.9% / +15.8% | 67.3% / +25.8% | 64.1% / +19.9% |
| 2024-2026 final | 61.7% / +15.4% | 62.4% / +16.7% | 66.6% / +24.6% | 64.0% / +19.7% |

Stand-in = the player's last-10 average (or, smarter, his career average in this position), offset so a
blind over wins half the time. The edge is largest when a player's role changed from his last game (tackles
+35% against +22% with the role unchanged, final set), which is exactly what a book with Tuesday's list also
sees. A leakage test confirms no game's own stats reach its features. Same reading as AFL disposals: a better
reading of form beats a plain average by a lot; real books may price better than any stand-in, so the true
figure can only be smaller and may be zero. Check which AU books even offer NRL run-metre and tackle lines
(the probe answers this).

## Tools ready for the paper season

- `python -m nrl.price.card nrl/cache games.csv [teams.csv]`: our fair prices for every game (h2h, line,
  total) and every named player (anytime, 2+, first try), at the open (last game's 17) or with Tuesday's lists.
- `bash nrl/build.sh`: rebuilds everything from the release.
