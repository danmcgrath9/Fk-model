# Credits: the rules and the budget (founder, 12 Nov 2026)

From 12 Nov 2026 the plan is Form King Pro: **30,000 bundled credits a month, resetting on the 12th, forfeited at the
reset**, plus **5,000 top-up credits that never expire** and are spent only once the bundle is gone (kept as a reserve:
the ledger leaves them out, so the 1,000 balance floor stops unattended runs before they are touched). Founder: "we have
to make 30,000 last for all state pulls ... we did alot of shit where I would just ask you to pull results etc multiple
times because I knew we had a ton of credits." And: "I don't want them lean, I want to make sure the model is
flourishing." So the model gets every race it can use, and nothing is pulled twice without a reason.

## The rules (agreed 12 Nov 2026)
1. **Results come from the 3am jobs only** (fk results for Victoria, fk all states at 3:30am for the rest). No extra
   results pulls, and no re-pulling to chase a missing Betfair SP: a gap at Form King's end is not fixed by asking
   again (Ballarat 11 Nov races 6-8: a re-pull cost 22 credits and found nothing).
2. **Pulls start later, at 11:15am Melbourne** (founder: "the riders don't always get done, but we can start the
   pulls later, lets say 11:15am"). The other states' races are pulled at 11:15am on race day, riders in.
3. **One odds refresh per meeting**, at the 9:30am re-price.
4. **Speedmaps once per meeting**, at the pull the meeting is priced from; re-pulls skip them.
5. **No research or back-fill pull without the founder's OK on a credit figure first**, and a daily spend line in the
   morning message.
- **No first-time horse profiles outside Victoria.** A profile adds the benchmark figures for runs 6-10, which the model
  has never read (it is a 5-run model: the race card carries every runner's 5 benchmarked runs and whole career). For
  the other states it would be about 24,000 credits in the first month and about 14,000 a month after. Whether
  Victoria keeps them (about 4,000 a month, for the form pages only) is the founder's call.

## What things cost

results), 1 a day (meeting list). Volumes from the national archive (Dave, Oct 2026): all of Australia about 1,490
races and 195 meetings a month; Victoria about 320 and 40; the other states about 1,180 and 155.
