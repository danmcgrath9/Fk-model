# When TAB opens each meeting's market, every state (23 Nov 2026)

From Betwatch's record of TAB's first price on every runner, 3,193 meetings, 1 Jul 2025 to 16 Nov 2026 (scratchpad
nat.npz, the national pull behind night/r9). Melbourne time. A meeting's open is its earliest race's first TAB price,
as Betwatch first saw it (TAB's true open can be a little earlier). "9 in 10 after" is the time only one meeting in
ten opened before: the latest a sheet can be written and still be safe for nearly every meeting of that kind.
Acceptance (final fields) times are not in this data: fk-fields-watch.yml records them for the week of 23-30 Nov.
