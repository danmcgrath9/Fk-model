# Sending rules

**Nothing is sent without the founder's "send" on the exact final text (founder, 27 Oct 2026: "Never do that again, always ask send").**

Applies to every email, message, reply, tweet and bet. An instruction to send something ("ask him the issue", "send it about the ones that didn't fire") is a request for a draft, not permission to send it. Show the final text, wait for "send", then send exactly that text.

Broken on 27 Oct: a follow-up to BetMatic support about the Warrnambool Market Open orders was sent without the founder seeing the wording first.
