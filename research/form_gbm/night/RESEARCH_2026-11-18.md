# 18 Nov 2026: four hours on the 9am list, and every state without the model

Founder, Wed 18 Nov 9:10am: "You have unlimited research time for 4 hours, do with it what you will please and get us
sharper, make us more money." Nothing live was changed. Every CALL is the founder's decision.
Scripts and their outputs are in `r9/` (run from the night work folder: `python3.13 r9/<script>.py`).

Why the 9am list: it is the only bet with guaranteed size. From 9am race day every bookmaker must lay a bet to win
$1,000 ($2,000 metro) in Victoria, NSW, Queensland and WA (2pm for a night meeting). The open book makes +74% on paper
and takes $150 on TAB; the 9am list can take a full bet at every bookmaker.

## What it found (the final word is Parts 5 and 6; Parts 1 to 4 are the first cut and their money figures are too high)

1. **THE MORNING'S FIRST RESULT WAS WRONG, AND THE SECOND SOURCE CAUGHT IT (Part 5).** The first cut (Parts 1 to 4) said
   the live 9am rule made +15% to +36% and a horse on both lists +64%. It inferred late scratchings from price moves.
   The national Betwatch pull holds the exact minute each horse was scratched, and re-running on that found two things
   the first cut could not see. It had missed 138 of 675 real late scratchings and counted 192 that had gone before
   9am. And nine races on two washed-out days (Geelong 4 Jun, Ballarat 12 Jul 2026) had up to eight horses come out
   45 to 100 minutes before 9am while the prices in the feed at 9:00 had not been re-framed: books of 0.51 to 0.99
   against a usual 1.28, so every runner left read as 20% to 90% over the odds. Those nine races were 222 bets on 36
   runners and +$27.7k, a third of the profit, and none of it could be bet without the deduction.
2. **ON THE CLEAN TEST THE 9AM RULE IS POSITIVE AND NOT PROVEN (Part 5).** Victoria, Nov 2025 to Nov 2026, exact
   scratching times, races with a whole 9am book, a bet to win the limit with every major at 1.05x the fair price:

   Every version made its money in Jul to Nov 2026 and lost a little in the eight months before (fitted: -6% then
   +55%). The four months before the test (Jul to Oct 2025, used only for fitting until now) were run last and were
   up on every version with our price in it: fitted +21.9% on 199 bets, the live list as built +33.2% on 53. So

   summer and autumn between them down: three periods, so a pattern to watch and nothing more. The fitted rule finds five times the bets of the live list at about the same return. More weight on our
   price than 0.28 gives it back. Honest size: somewhere between nothing and +18%, most likely positive.
3. **NO SPLIT OF THE LIST HELD (Part 5).** The first cut's "on both lists, +64%" is -17% on the clean test (164 bets);
   on the fitted list only is +33% (598 bets). What pointed the same way in both cuts, and is a lead to watch on the
   shadow list, not a rule: races jumping within six hours of 9am (+27% to +69%, against about nothing later),
   country and provincial (+42%, metro -3%), and our price shorter than the market's (+15% to +88%, against +5%).
4. **WITHOUT OUR MODEL THERE IS NO 9AM BET, IN ANY STATE (Part 6).** 24,582 races, every state, Jul 2025 to Nov 2026,
   weights fitted on Victoria and tested everywhere else: the market and the move made +0.5% on 572 bets. The one
   thing that showed: a single major 5% or more over what the other six imply made +25.6% on 385 bets outside
   Victoria (11 of 17 months up, mostly Sportsbet and Betr), about one bet a day across the country. A paper trial at
   most: one bookmaker alone can be a late price in the feed.
5. **THE BETFAIR PRICE-ONLY RULES DO NOT HOLD ON FIVE TIMES THE RACES (Part 6).** 221,625 runners with a Betfair SP,
   every state, 16 months, 8% commission: all six rules lose overall (-1.5% to -6.3%) and all six lose outside
   Victoria. Victoria's three small pluses (+7%, +4%, +3%) are what one state in eight gets by chance. They stay
   tracked every day, as the founder asked; nothing here says bet them.
6. **PRICES LAST, AND LATER LISTS ADD LITTLE (Part 4, and 5).** 88% of the list is still at the take price at 9:10,
   80% at 9:30, 72% at 10:00 (a fact about prices; it stands). New bets found by re-making the list later: 95 bets
   -8% four hours out, 46 bets +22% one hour out, 74 bets +14% ten minutes out. Nothing to build on.
7. **WHAT WAS BUILT.** The whole-book guard, in the verify step (observe: it flags a 9am list in a race whose prices
   add to under 1.08 of a book) and in the shadow list (leaves the race off and names it). The shadow list lost its
   grade. `scripts/betwatch_national.py` pulls every state with exact scratching times.

**CALLs (the founder's):** (1) the 9am list: switch it to the fitted rule at the same stake, keep the live rule, or
hold real bets until the shadow list shows it; (2) the whole-book guard on the live list (in observe now);
(3) Betwatch after Friday: it is what caught the error, and the history to 16 Nov is already pulled.

**THE LESSON, for the next test of anything on a morning price:** a price in a feed is a claim that it could be bet.
Check the race's book adds up before believing any runner in it, and settle scratchings from their recorded time,
never from how prices moved. Two tests in a row (13 Nov and this morning) reported a 9am edge that was the same
missing horse seen two ways.

## Part 1. The correction: late scratchings were missing from every 9am test (r9/build.py, common.py)

The Betwatch pull holds every runner that had a price, with a scratched flag; the model dataset holds the runners that
ran. Matching the two: 855 runners were flagged scratched, held a 9am price and were still being re-priced by a
bookmaker after 9am race day. They are in 647 of 5,178 races (one race in eight). Another 12,228 scratched runners had
no price move after 9am and are taken as out before 9am (emergencies and the morning's scratchings).
Handled from here on: a late scratching is IN the race's 9am market (our chance for it = its market share, the rest
scaled to fit), a bet on it is void, a winner in its race is paid after a deduction equal to its share of the 9am
market (median 9c, mean 12c in the affected races, close to what the founder reports from TAB), and a race where a
runner that ran has no 9am price is dropped. The old test, on the same 13 test months and the same rule:

The old test's extra 1,874 bookie bets were every runner in a race with a late scratching, bet as if the scratched
