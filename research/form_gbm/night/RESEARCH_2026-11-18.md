# 18 Nov 2026: four hours on the 9am list, and every state without the model

Founder, Wed 18 Nov 9:10am: "You have unlimited research time for 4 hours, do with it what you will please and get us
sharper, make us more money." Nothing live was changed. Every CALL is the founder's decision.
Scripts and their outputs are in `r9/` (run from the night work folder: `python3.13 r9/<script>.py`).

Why the 9am list: it is the only bet with guaranteed size. From 9am race day every bookmaker must lay a bet to win
$1,000 ($2,000 metro) in Victoria, NSW, Queensland and WA (2pm for a night meeting). The open book makes +74% on paper
and takes $150 on TAB; the 9am list can take a full bet at every bookmaker.

## What it found, in order of money

1. **EVERY EARLIER TEST OF THE 9AM RULE WAS WRONG IN THE SAME WAY, AND THE RULE IS BETTER THAN THEY SAID (Part 1).**
   The model dataset holds only runners that ran. A horse scratched after 9am was in the 9am market, so leaving it out
   made every other runner in its race look like an overlay: 73% of the old test's bets were these phantoms, about
   break-even, drowning the real ones. With late scratchings put back (855 of them, 647 races, bets on them void,
   winners paid after the deduction) the live rule's bets fall from 2,560 to 686 over 13 months and its return rises
   from +4.7% to +15.2%.
2. **THE RULE IS REAL AND IT IS RARE (Part 2).** About one runner every two race days with a major at the take price.
   On the seven majors alone, 13 test months: +$41k to +$133k depending on the version, +15% to +50%, every version
   up. Resampling race days, the chance of a losing 13 months is under 1%; a losing month is 2 to 6 in 13.
3. **A RUNNER ON BOTH LISTS IS THE BET (Part 3).** The live list (our price blended with the BEST price now) and a
   second list fitted on the market's average and the move since the open: on both, +64% on 407 bookie bets
   (+$100.6k); on one only, about +12%. CALL: add the second list as grades A and B (`blend_9am_v2.py`, shadow, built).
4. **THE MOVE SINCE THE OPEN IS NOT FULLY IN THE 9AM PRICE (2.2).** A horse that has firmed since the open wins more
   often than its 9am price says; a drifter less (weight -0.22, t about 3 on 3,800 races, stable in every monthly fit).
5. **BET IT BY 10AM AND NEVER CHASE A DRIFTER (Part 4).** 88% of the list is still at the take price ten minutes after
   9:00, 80% at 9:30, 72% at 10:00, and the return holds for an hour (+30%); after two hours it halves. A horse that
   only reaches the take price by drifting after 9am lost 16% on 5,193 runners. A list re-made later in the day with
   the market then finds nothing new that pays.
6. **NIGHT MEETINGS AND LATE RACES ADD NOTHING (2.5).** Night meetings: 79 bets, -0.4% (and the limits do not start
   until 2pm). Races within six hours of 9am made +49% to +72%; eight hours or more, +1%.
7. **OUR PRICE HOLDS ITS WEIGHT ALL DAY (4.3).** About 0.25 against the market from 9am until 30 minutes before the
   jump; 0.13 at ten minutes; 0.06 at two. The market only catches up with the model in the last half hour.

## Part 1. The correction: late scratchings were missing from every 9am test (r9/build.py, common.py)

The Betwatch pull holds every runner that had a price, with a scratched flag; the model dataset holds the runners that
ran. Matching the two: 855 runners were flagged scratched, held a 9am price and were still being re-priced by a
bookmaker after 9am race day. They are in 647 of 5,178 races (one race in eight). Another 12,228 scratched runners had
no price move after 9am and are taken as out before 9am (emergencies and the morning's scratchings).
Handled from here on: a late scratching is IN the race's 9am market (our chance for it = its market share, the rest
scaled to fit), a bet on it is void, a winner in its race is paid after a deduction equal to its share of the 9am
market (median 9c, mean 12c in the affected races, close to what the founder reports from TAB), and a race where a
runner that ran has no 9am price is dropped. The old test, on the same 13 test months and the same rule:

| | runners | bookie bets | winners | staked | profit | ROI | months up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| THE OLD TEST of the live rule: late scratchings left out of the race, no deduction | 589 | 2560 | 116 | $903,504 | $+42,590 | +4.7% | 5 of 13 |
| LIVE 0.20 ours + 1.00 TAB | 240 | 686 | 50 | $267,455 | $+40,681 | +15.2% | 8 of 13 |
| 0.20 ours + 1.00 median majors | 174 | 508 | 41 | $194,357 | $+92,368 | +47.5% | 7 of 13 |

The old test's extra 1,874 bookie bets were every runner in a race with a late scratching, bet as if the scratched
horse's chance had never been in the book. t_9am_bookies.py, t_9am_betwatch.py, t_9am_drift.py and t_blend_bsp.py all
share it; their counts are too high and their returns too low. The live list is not affected: at 8:35am the field it
prices still holds the horse.
